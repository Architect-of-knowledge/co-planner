# Taste: what the user likes and cuts, and why

Read at the start of every co-planner session to aim its questions and its 3rd-knowledge search. Entries come from the user's picks **and their notes**, which often say more than the option they picked, and from the review after each wave (what they actually used). A preference seen 2–3 times is proposed as a rule in `design-rules.md`.
